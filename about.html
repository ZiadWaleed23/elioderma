Our Story

Elio began with a simple observation: aesthetic professionals were choosing between suppliers built for luxury retail or clinical distribution, but rarely both. We built a single portfolio — dermal fillers, injectables, skin boosters, professional skincare, beauty tools and aesthetic equipment — organized around how a clinic actually works.
